PrivacySupport

Free, and stays free

Steps and goal

Steps, distance, flights and active energy, a daily goal ring, this week's trail and streaks.

History

7 days to 2 years. Tap a day for steps by hour, your walks and that day's paths on a map.

Record paths

Walks, runs and hikes with GPS on iPhone or Apple Watch, saved to Apple Health.

Voice cues

Split times and a "goal reached" cue in your headphones while you record.

Widgets and Watch

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Watch app and complications.

Travel aware

Days abroad are counted midnight to midnight in local time, automatically.

Your routes

Every saved walk on a map. Dragging the chart for elevation and heart rate along the path is Pro.

Suggest a loop

Ask for 20 minutes or a few miles from where you are. Stops come from a walk you already took. Save favorites on the phone.

StepOrbit Pro

Insights

How many walks you take, when, on which days, and what gets you to your goal.

Your weather effect

How rain, snow, wind and heat change your steps, using the weather where you walked.

Look-backs

Weekly and monthly reports, plus a morning heads-up: "Rain from 2 pm, walk this morning."

Along the path

Splits, elevation and heart rate at the point you touch on the chart.

Missed walks

Find walks you didn't record in your iPhone's motion history and save them to Health.

Smart detection

Already walking? Start recording from when you set off.

Pro is $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year with a 2-week free trial. Subscriptions are handled by Apple and can be cancelled any time in Settings.

Private by design

No account, no servers, no analytics. Health and route data stays on your devices and in Apple Health. Weather and place names are looked up with Apple's services using rounded locations only. Read the privacy policy.
